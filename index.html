Bio

Hi there! I am a third-year PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, Hong Kong SAR. I received my M.S. degree in 2023 from the School of Computer Science & the School of Artificial Intelligence, Optics, and Electronics (iOPEN), Northwestern Polytechnical University, Xi'an, Shaanxi. I received my B.S. degree in 2020 from the School of Computer Science, Northwestern Polytechnical University.

🔬 Research Interests
  • Computer Vision
  • MLLMs / VLMs
  • Egocentric Vision
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Multi-modal Alignment
  • Embodied AI

I am actively seeking collaboration opportunities in the fields of Computer Vision, Embodied AI, and Multi-modal Large Language Models. I warmly invite researchers and practitioners with shared interests to connect with me via email.

Furthermore, I am currently exploring opportunities for both a short-term internship and a long-term research position, and would be delighted to discuss potential openings.
